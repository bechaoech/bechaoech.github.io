Home Flavors

Wherever life takes us, we carry with us the comforting flavors of home. 🍲

In The Early Days

When we first arrived in the U.S., everything felt new — a new place, a new life, and a whole new rhythm to adjust to. One of the hardest parts was food. Both of us have always loved Vietnamese noodle dishes, but finding that familiar taste here wasn’t easy at all. Back in Vietnam, we rarely had to cook for ourselves. We often ate out, and if we had meals at home, our mothers were the ones who cooked for us. But in this new country, it was just the two of us — which meant learning to take care of everything on our own, from figuring out where to shop to cooking our own meals. In America, everything feels far apart. During those first weeks, we didn’t know where to find Vietnamese food, or even which markets sold the ingredients we needed to recreate the flavors of home. It was challenging at first, but slowly, we learned. We discovered which Asian markets carried the right sauces, noodles, and herbs that reminded us of home. Even though we can’t always find every single ingredient, we’ve managed to gather enough to make our small kitchen feel like home again. Here, I want to share some of the dishes I’ve cooked for my husband — meals from the early days of his PhD journey, simple Vietnamese dishes (and sometimes others) that carried with them a little taste of home.

Our Taste of Home

When we first moved to the U.S., trying to cook Vietnamese noodle dishes was quite a challenge. I experimented again and again, hoping to bring back the familiar flavors of the meals we used to enjoy every day in Vietnam. The dishes weren’t always perfect, but watching my husband smile as he ate made every effort worthwhile. Seeing him well-fed and a little less homesick always warms my heart. With a happy stomach, he can now focus wholeheartedly on his PhD — and I feel so grateful to walk beside him on that journey.
